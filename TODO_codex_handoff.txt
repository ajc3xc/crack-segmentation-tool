### Expected impact on results

`dt_trench_rgb` = RGB trench + color anomaly, no depth. Sits between `dt_trench` (RGB, no color, no depth) and `dt_trench_color_depth` (RGB + color + depth). Its RS3 score will likely be between those two, confirming depth is the more impactful signal. If it outperforms `dt_trench_color_depth`, that would be the more interesting finding — depth hurts when combined with color.
